## 🌟 Overview

**AgriAI / CDD-AI** is a futuristic, interactive 3D scrollytelling presentation website built with **Three.js**, **GSAP**, and the **Web Speech API**. It is designed for classroom defense, mini-projects, and seminar presentations.

A 3D holographic **AI Student Co-Presenter** with graduation cap and animated smart visor explains every stage of the project step-by-step with synchronized text and optional natural voice narration.

## 🎮 Presentation Controls & Shortcuts

| Shortcut | Action | Description |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **`Space`** or **`→`** | Next Stage | Advances to the next slide and moves the 3D camera smoothly. |
| **`←`** | Previous Stage | Returns to the previous presentation slide. |
| **`L`** | Virtual Laser Pointer | Turns on a glowing laser dot that tracks your mouse across 3D objects. |
| **`V`** | Voice Narration | Toggles the AI Student Narrator speech voice ON/OFF. |
| **`F`** | Fullscreen Mode | Enters borderless presentation view (ideal for classroom projectors). |
| **`1` - `8`** | Quick Jump | Jumps directly to any of the 8 presentation stages. |

## 🧭 The 8 Presentation Stages

1. **Stage 1: Overview & Vision (Hero)**: Glowing cybernetic Earth, orbital neural arcs, and co-presenter welcome.
2. **Stage 2: The Problem Statement**: Agricultural challenges (heat stress, drought, early blight, manual inspection delays).
3. **Stage 3: Multi-Modal IoT & Hardware Pipeline**: 3D Exploded schematic of ESP32 MCU, DHT22 sensor, capacitive soil probe, camera, and 5V relay module.
4. **Stage 4: Dual-Core AI/ML Engine**: 3D CNN deep learning visualizer (MobileNet feature maps, dense layers, output nodes) + Random Forest stress predictor.
5. **Stage 5: Live Interactive 3D Classroom Simulator**: 
   - Interactive 3D tomato plant with dynamic leaf discoloration shaders.
   - Live sliders for Temperature, Soil Moisture, and Humidity.
   - Closed-loop automated 5V water pump with animated water particle jet spray!
   - 4 Quick presets for classroom demonstration (*Normal, Drought, Early Blight, Late Blight*).
6. **Stage 6: Scientific Novelty**: Comparison matrix highlighting why multi-modal sensor fusion outperforms single-photo classifiers.
7. **Stage 7: Future Scope**: 3D Autonomous agricultural drone surveying topographic farm contours with laser raster beams.
8. **Stage 8: Final Defense & Q&A**: Key defense arguments for professors, limitation acknowledgments, and celebration confetti.
